# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials, cookies, or private media.
- Accept maintainer decisions and help keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks,
doxxing, spam, and encouraging unauthorized access or copyright infringement
are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this policy is violated.
Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ShootDoRi). Security reports
must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
